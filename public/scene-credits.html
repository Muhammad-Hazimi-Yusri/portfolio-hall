AVVR listening room

The listening space and sound demo are original portfolio illustrations, with a screenshot of the AVVR university application. The project's research used the S3A Audio-Visual Scene Analysis dataset (DOI 10.15126/surreydata.00812228). Its raw room model and 360° photograph are not distributed here; see the dataset access terms.

Dataset references: Kim, de Campos and Hilton (3DV 2016), on room-layout and material-attribute estimation; Kim and colleagues (AES 2017), on spherical-camera acoustic room modelling. Full references are on the dataset page.

Semantic reconstruction also builds on Kim and colleagues' 2021 work. Hazimi's contribution was the university project's software and VR integration. The optional sound demo is a separate browser illustration.

Island scenery

Elevation samples around Rùm, Eigg and Canna from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed 23 September 2026. The samples are reduced, resized, vertically exaggerated and rearranged for the hall. This is a fictional landscape.
